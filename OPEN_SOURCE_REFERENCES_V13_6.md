# Open Source References V13.6 (Strategy Self-Evaluation & ML Research)

AISTOCK v13.6 integrates and references industry-standard quantitative finance & ML frameworks:

1. **Microsoft Qlib**
   - Reference: https://github.com/microsoft/qlib
   - Usage: Off-line research bridge, feature pipeline structure, walk-forward validation dataset schema.

2. **vectorbt / PyAlgoTrade**
   - Reference: https://github.com/polakoby/vectorbt
   - Usage: Expectancy R, profit factor, win rate, and drawdown mathematical definitions for strategy performance evaluation.

3. **TradingView Lightweight Charts™**
   - Reference: https://github.com/tradingview/lightweight-charts
   - Usage: Unified candlestick, volume, and forecast series rendering.
